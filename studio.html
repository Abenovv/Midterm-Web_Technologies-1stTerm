About SIDEQUEST

We are a team of three students from IT-2511. SIDEQUEST is our game development studio concept for Web Technologies 1.

We chose this topic because we enjoy games and wanted to introduce several ideas in one website.

What we want to do next

For the next project stages, we would like to add more game information, publish development updates, and improve the community features.
